## Purpose

A caller who reads the docstring of `peek` or `pop` learns the error an empty queue really raises
(`docs/product-specs/f1.md`).

Steps:

- HeapPriorityQueue().peek() and pop() on an empty queue raise IndexError.
- SortedPriorityQueue().peek() and pop() on an empty queue raise IndexError.
- peek(None) and pop(None) on an empty queue return None.
- The docstrings of peek and pop name IndexError.
